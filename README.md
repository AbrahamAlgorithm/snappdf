# snappdf

![snappdf Cover](.github/assets/banner.png)

> Your files never leave your device

snappdf is a free, open-source PDF toolkit that runs entirely in your browser. It helps you merge, split, sign, scan and convert PDFs, without ever uploading your files.

## Features

- **Image to PDF**: Turn photos, screenshots and scans (iPhone HEIC included) into one PDF. On a phone, the camera auto-crops and straightens the page, like a scanner app.
- **Merge PDF**: Combine PDFs into one, in the order you choose.
- **Sign PDF**: Draw or upload a signature and place it anywhere on the page.
- **Split PDF**: Pick the pages you want and save them as a new PDF.
- **Text to PDF**: Type, paste or open a text file and save it as a tidy PDF.
- **Word to PDF**: Turn a Word document (.docx) into a PDF that looks the same, and see every page before you download.
- **CSV & JSON**: Convert to Excel, or JSON to CSV.
- **Works Offline**: Installs like an app and keeps working with no internet.
- **Privacy Focused**: Every file is processed on your device and never uploaded.

## Setup & How it Works

1. **Open**: Go to snappdf, no install and no sign-up needed.
2. **Pick**: Choose a tool from the workspace.
3. **Add**: Drop in your file, or take a photo on your phone.
4. **Download**: Everything happens in your browser, so your file is ready to save and was never uploaded.
